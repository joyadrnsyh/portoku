Overview

TechCorp needed a website that reflected their position as an industry leader in AI solutions. The old site was static and uninspiring; the new one is alive with motion.

The Challenge

Implementing decent WebGL effects without crashing low-end laptops. We had to optimize the 3D assets significantly.

The Solution

  • Three.js for the hero background.
  • GSAP for scroll-triggered animations.
  • Lazy loading for heavy assets.

Tech Stack

Three.js GSAP WebGL
